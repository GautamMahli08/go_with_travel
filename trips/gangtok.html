Gangtok

Gangtok & East Sikkim

Monasteries, mountain lakes and the vibrant capital of Sikkim, set against the snows of Kanchenjunga.

₹16,999 per person5 Days / 4 NightsBagdogra · Gangtok · Tsomgo Lake · Rumtek
DAY 1
Arrive Bagdogra / NJP – drive to Gangtok

Your LAVIGO driver meets you at Bagdogra Airport or New Jalpaiguri station for the scenic drive along the Teesta river to Gangtok (approx. 4–5 hours). Evening on the pedestrian promenade of MG Marg.

DAY 2
Tsomgo Lake & Baba Mandir

A high-altitude excursion to the glacial Tsomgo (Changu) Lake at 12,300 ft and the Baba Harbhajan Singh Memorial. Nathu La Pass can be added on request (open to Indian nationals only; permits arranged in advance).

DAY 3
Gangtok sightseeing

Visit Rumtek Monastery, Enchey Monastery, Do Drul Chorten and the Namgyal Institute of Tibetology, then take in sweeping views from Ganesh Tok, Hanuman Tok and Tashi Viewpoint.

DAY 4
Banjhakri Falls & leisure

A relaxed morning at Banjhakri Falls and the flower exhibition centre, with the afternoon free for local cafés, handicrafts and the Gangtok ropeway.

DAY 5
Journey home

Drive back to Bagdogra or NJP for your onward journey, or continue to Lachen & Lachung in North Sikkim.
